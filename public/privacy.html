Privacy notice

Private by design

CropRotate PDF edits documents locally in your browser. Your PDF and any password you enter are processed only in the active browser tab. The app does not upload them or keep a server-side copy.

Your document stays under your control. Edits are temporary until you download the result. Closing or reloading the tab discards the working session. You keep ownership of your files and outputs.

What stays in the tab

The selected PDF bytes, generated previews, password, crop and rotation choices, and undo history live in browser memory for the active editing session. CropRotate PDF does not use cookies, local storage, session storage, IndexedDB, analytics, or a server database to retain those document contents.

When data leaves the tab

  • A new PDF is saved only when you explicitly choose to download it.
  • The original file is not modified by the app.
  • Reloading, closing the tab, or opening another PDF ends the current working session.

Hosting and ordinary connection data

The static app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like other web hosts, GitHub may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address, request time, requested page, browser details, and referring page. Your browser may cache the app's code and visual assets for performance. Neither of these actions sends the PDF you selected to the app's host.

Things outside this app

Browser extensions, operating-system memory or swap behavior, download history, cloud-synced download folders, and any software that opens the exported file are controlled by your browser, device, or third-party services—not by CropRotate PDF. Use a browser and device you trust when working with sensitive documents.

Your files remain yours

You keep control and ownership of documents you open and outputs you create. CropRotate PDF does not claim ownership of them.
