Private PDF editor and page organizer

Crop, rotate, split, merge and reorder PDFs privately

CropRotate PDF is a free browser tool for precision cropping, page rotation, automatic margin trimming, local PDF splitting and merging, page reordering and extraction. The app processes selected PDFs locally in this browser tab instead of uploading the documents to a server.

Your document stays under your control

The app does not keep a server-side copy of your PDFs or passwords. Edits remain temporary until you download a new file, and closing or reloading the tab discards the working session. You keep ownership of your files and outputs.
