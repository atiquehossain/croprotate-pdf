Private PDF annotator, editor and page organizer

Annotate, visually sign and organize PDFs privately

CropRotate PDF is a free browser tool for pen, highlighter, text, date, marks, lines, arrows and shapes; drawn or typed visual signatures and initials; precision cropping, page rotation, local splitting and merging, reordering and extraction. The app processes selected PDFs locally in this browser tab instead of uploading the documents to a server.

Your document stays under your control

The app does not keep a server-side copy of your PDFs, annotations, signatures or passwords, and it does not use analytics. Edits remain temporary until you download a new file, and closing or reloading the tab discards the working session. You keep ownership of your files and outputs.
