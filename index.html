Private PDF crop and rotation tool

Crop and rotate PDFs privately in your browser

CropRotate PDF is a free browser tool for precision cropping, page rotation, automatic margin trimming and batch edits. The app processes the selected PDF locally in this browser tab instead of uploading the document to a server.

Your document stays under your control

The app does not keep a server-side copy of your PDF or password. Edits remain temporary until you download a new file, and closing or reloading the tab discards the working session. You keep ownership of your files and outputs.
